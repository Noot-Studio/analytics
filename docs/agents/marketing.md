# Marketing & Positioning

## Channels

1. **s&box Discord & Forums**: Primary community, direct developer engagement
2. **Reddit**: r/sandbox, r/gamedev, r/indiegamedev
3. **YouTube**: Tutorial videos, "Analytics for s&box" series
4. **Hacker News**: Launch post, Show HN
5. **Content SEO**: "s&box analytics", "game analytics for Source 2" blog posts

## Launch Sequence

- **Private Beta**: Invite s&box developers personally, gather feedback
- **Public Beta**: Open signup, no credit card required, generous free tier
- **Public Launch**: Coordinated announcement across all channels, limited-time Pro discount

## Positioning

> "Analytics built for s&box developers — not marketers."

Emphasize game-native events, privacy, and performance.
